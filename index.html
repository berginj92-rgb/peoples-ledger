The People's Ledger

What government is actually doing, from official records.

  • How every federal MP and senator voted, with the official bill PDFs and Hansard record behind each vote.
  • Election promises and what has happened to them, with sources.
  • The 2026 Victorian election: key dates, every district and region, sitting members and past results from the Victorian Electoral Commission and the Parliament of Victoria.
  • Compare your views with how the parties actually voted on real bills.

Free, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Support

Found something wrong, or need help? Every fact in the app links to its official source, and we fix errors as soon as we can.

Report an error or ask a question (needs a free GitHub account).

Where the data comes from

Parliament of Australia (aph.gov.au and Hansard), the Federal Register of Legislation, the Australian Electoral Commission, the Victorian Electoral Commission and the Parliament of Victoria. Federal Hansard divisions are as parsed by the OpenAustralia Foundation.

Privacy policy

The People's Ledger is not affiliated with any parliament, electoral commission or political party.
